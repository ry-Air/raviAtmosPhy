About Me

Welcome to my academic website. I am Ravi Yadav, a researcher at the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune.


My research focuses on Atmospheric Chemistry, air quality, aerosols, trace gases, and their interactions with weather and climate systems. I work on understanding atmospheric processes using observations, modelling, and data analysis.

Research Interests

  • Air Quality and Pollution
  • Aerosol Processes
  • Trace Gases and Reactive Species
  • Climate-Chemistry Interactions
  • Environmental Data Analysis
