About Me

I am a Project Scientist III at the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune, working in Atmospheric Chemistry, Air Quality, Trace Gases, VOCs, Ozone Chemistry, and Atmospheric Modelling.

My research focuses on atmospheric composition, VOC measurements, air pollution climatology, long-range transport, simulation chamber studies, and chemical mechanisms governing tropospheric ozone formation.
