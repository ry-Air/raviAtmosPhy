About Me

I am an Atmospheric Physicist working on atmospheric chemistry, air quality, climate interactions, VOC oxidation processes, secondary organic aerosol formation, and atmospheric simulation chamber studies.

My research combines laboratory experiments, simulation chamber studies, advanced analytical instrumentation, and field observations to understand atmospheric processes affecting air quality and climate.

Research

Atmospheric Chemistry

Understanding chemical processes that control the composition and transformation of the atmosphere.

Air Quality

Investigating atmospheric pollutants and their impacts on urban and regional air quality.

Volatile Organic Compounds

Studying VOC sources, oxidation, chemical transformation and their atmospheric impacts.

Secondary Organic Aerosol

Investigating SOA formation and atmospheric processing of organic compounds.

Photochemical Processes

Understanding sunlight-driven chemical reactions in the atmosphere.

Atmospheric Simulation Chambers

Using controlled laboratory experiments to investigate atmospheric oxidation and aerosol formation.
