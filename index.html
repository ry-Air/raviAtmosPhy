About Me

I am an Atmospheric Physicist working on atmospheric chemistry, air quality, climate interactions, VOC oxidation processes, secondary organic aerosol formation, and atmospheric simulation chamber studies. My research combines laboratory experiments, simulation chamber studies, advanced analytical instrumentation, and field observations to understand atmospheric processes affecting air quality and climate.

Research Interests

  • Atmospheric Chemistry
  • Air Pollution & Air Quality
  • Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs)
  • Secondary Organic Aerosol Formation
  • Photochemical Processes
  • Climate–Atmosphere Interactions
  • Atmospheric Simulation Chambers
